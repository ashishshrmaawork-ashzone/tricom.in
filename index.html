Have another question?

ISO certification is independent confirmation that a management system meets the requirements of a particular standard. The right standard depends on your activities, customer expectations, tender requirements and business goals. We help you assess that fit.

ISO 9001 focuses on quality, ISO 14001 on environmental management, ISO 45001 on occupational health and safety, ISO 27001 on information security, ISO 22000 on food safety and ISO 50001 on energy management. A consultation helps narrow the options for your business.

The timeline depends on your organisation’s size, scope, existing processes and readiness, as well as the certification body’s audit schedule. We assess your situation first and then agree a realistic implementation plan.

Depending on the agreed scope, support can include gap analysis, documentation, implementation guidance, team awareness, internal audit preparation, management review and corrective action support.

No. Tricom Consultants provides consultancy, implementation and audit preparation support. An independent certification body conducts the certification audit and makes the certification decision.

Fees depend on the standard, business size, number of locations, existing systems and level of support required. Request a consultation for a proposal tailored to your scope. Consultancy and independent certification body fees should be clearly identified.
